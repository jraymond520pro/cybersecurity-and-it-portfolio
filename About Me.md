About Me
Curious about how it all clicks together.

I've spent the last several years exploring technology through hands-on experimentation, self-directed learning, and an unhealthy tendency to keep asking "why?" until I find the answer.

What started as an interest in computers grew into a fascination with systems. Linux, cybersecurity, networking, virtualization, web development, homelab infrastructure, and security operations all became pieces of a much larger picture.

I currently run Arch Linux, maintain a homelab environment, and actively explore security operations concepts through technologies such as Wazuh, virtualized lab environments, network analysis, and infrastructure troubleshooting. Whether I'm investigating a virtualization issue, deploying a website, analyzing logs, researching a CVE, or standing up new services in my lab, my goal is always the same: understand how the pieces fit together.

I enjoy taking things apart, figuring out how they work, documenting what I learn, and building practical experience through real projects rather than tutorials alone.

This repository documents the adventures, investigations, failures, fixes, and lessons learned along the way.

I don't expect to know everything. I do expect that with curiosity, persistence, and enough experimentation, I can learn almost anything I set my mind to.
